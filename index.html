    About us

    Med Serve Health Care Clinic

    Med Serve is a health care clinic where patients can see a doctor, collect medicines from the pharmacy, get an ECG, have lab tests done, and use nebulisation and observation services in one place.

    Our aim is simple: friendly, reliable care for you and your family.
